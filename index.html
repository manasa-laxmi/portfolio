Hi, I'm Manasa.
I'm a final-year Computer Science student at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad, aspiring to build a career in Product Management. I enjoy turning ambiguous problems into meaningful products by understanding user needs, shaping product strategy, and measuring impact.
My interests lie at the intersection of Product Management, AI/ML, Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), and Data Analytics. I enjoy exploring product ideas, experimenting with AI-powered tools, and building products that are intuitive, scalable, and worth coming back to.

SentinelAI

05Research · HCI
How AI answers shape our decisions
When an AI hands you an answer, do you still think it through?

      
A survey on how far AI shapes everyday decisions. 57% said they think problems through on their own less often since using AI, and 71% let it shape study and career choices, yet only 36% said they trust its answers. This case study explores that gap between relying on AI and trusting it.

09Design · HCI
Navigation for visually impaired users
How do you guide someone through a space they can't see?

      
We empathised with users through research and blindfolded simulations, then iterated with a team of four. When distance-based directions proved confusing and audio failed in noise, we added distinct haptic patterns, a "Where am I" feature, a lost mode and indoor navigation.
